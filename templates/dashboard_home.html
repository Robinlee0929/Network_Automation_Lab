3-5 Minute Interview Demo Flow

  1. Open this dashboard landing page.
  2. Show the report index and latest overview from Reports.
  3. Show the safety checklist and command allowlist.
  4. Open the offline demo kit or demo commands.
  5. Explain that live device actions are guarded and not triggered by voice or AI directly.
